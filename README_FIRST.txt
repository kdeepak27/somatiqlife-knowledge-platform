SOMatiq Production Curriculum v1

WHAT TO COPY INTO YOUR REPOSITORY
---------------------------------
Copy ONLY the 17 CSV files inside:

  config/curricula/

into your repository's:

  config/curricula/

Do NOT copy the planning/ folder into config/curricula/.

The production curricula are numbered 01 through 17 so the upgraded generator
processes them in a predictable order.

IMPORTANT ABOUT MODULE IDs
--------------------------
This package intentionally preserves your established ID format:
  SLP-001, STR-001, LIF-001, etc.

Do NOT switch to SLP0001-style IDs now. BIO, NUT, and EXE already use the
hyphenated format, and changing conventions mid-project would create unnecessary
inconsistency and could break references.

PLANNING FILES
--------------
The planning/ folder contains:
  master_curriculum_v1.csv
  curriculum_manifest.csv
  validation_report.json

These are for management and review only. Keep them OUTSIDE config/curricula/.
