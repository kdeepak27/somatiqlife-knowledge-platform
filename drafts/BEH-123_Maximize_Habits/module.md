## Glossary
- **Basal ganglia:** A group of brain structures involved in controlling voluntary movements and habit formation.
- **Habit formation:** The process by which new behaviors become automatic through repetition and reinforcement.
- **Neuroplasticity:** The brain's ability to reorganize and form new neural connections in response to learning or experience.
- **Reinforcement:** The process of encouraging a behavior by rewarding its occurrence, increasing its frequency.
- **Cue-response relationship:** The triggering of an automatic behavior (response) by a specific environmental or internal signal (cue).
- **SMART goals:** A framework for setting objectives that are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.
- **Motivation:** The internal drive or desire to perform a behavior or achieve a goal.
- **Willpower:** The self-control strength used to overcome impulses and maintain goal-directed behaviors.
